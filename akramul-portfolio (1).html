About

Day to day, I keep the collection organized and accessible — shelving and reshelving books, applying call number and RFID labels, maintaining a quiet space for students to study, and helping them track down the resources they need.

Alongside that, I bring a background in cataloging standards (AACR2, MARC21) and library systems (Koha, DSpace) from earlier roles and training.

I'm especially interested in what AI means for cataloging in South Asian academic libraries — not as a replacement for professional judgment, but as a tool that raises new questions about accuracy, bias, and authenticity in how we organize knowledge.
